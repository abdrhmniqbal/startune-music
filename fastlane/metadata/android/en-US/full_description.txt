Startune Music is an open-source Android music player for local music and self-hosted libraries.

Play music stored on your device: MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV and M4A, with albums, artists, genres and folders, favorites, playlists, statistics and Top Songs. The player supports gapless playback, crossfade, custom transitions, queue controls, shuffle, repeat and a sleep timer.

Connect your own Navidrome or Jellyfin server to sync, stream and download music for offline listening. No account is needed for local playback.

Synced lyrics with translation and romanization, lyrics import and editing, album-art palettes that color the interface, and home screen widgets.

Back up preferences, playlists, favorites, lyrics and stats to a .starbk file and restore them later.

Last.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbling are opt-in only. The app has no ads and no analytics.
